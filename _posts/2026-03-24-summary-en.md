Its Covered List is a regulatory tool that identifies communications equipment and services determined to pose an unacceptable risk to US national security, restricting or prohibiting the use or import of listed items. The list is maintained by the FCC's Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau and is updated periodically to reflect new security assessments.
