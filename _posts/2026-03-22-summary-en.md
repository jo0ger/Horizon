## [Perseverance Finds Ancient Buried Delta on Mars](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/03/perseverances-radar-revealed-ancient-subsurface-river-delta-on-mars/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NASA's Perseverance rover detected an additional, more ancient buried river delta dozens of meters below the already known western delta in Mars' Jezero crater using its RIMFAX ground-penetrating radar. The detection data was collected between September 2023 and February 2024 while the rover traversed the crater's Margin unit. This discovery revises existing scientific understanding of Jezero crater's geological history and identifies a high-potential new target for astrobiologists searching for traces of ancient life on Mars. It also contributes valuable new data to planetary science research on Mars' ancient aquatic environments. The newly found delta is located in a region rich in magnesium carbonate, a mineral that researchers consider favorable for preserving chemical traces of ancient life. RIMFAX, the ground-penetrating radar used in the detection, can probe subsurface geologic features up to around 20 meters below the Martian surface.

**Background**: Perseverance is NASA's Mars rover that landed in Jezero crater in 2021, with a core mission to search for signs of ancient life on Mars. RIMFAX, short for the Radar Imager for Mars' Subsurface Experiment, is a ground-penetrating radar instrument carried by Perseverance that uses radio waves to map geologic structures hidden under the Martian surface. Jezero crater was chosen as Perseverance's landing site because it was already confirmed to host an ancient river delta formed billions of years ago when the crater held a lake, making it a prime location to search for ancient biosignatures.
