## [Moonshot AI Unveils Attention Residuals Technique](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Attention-Residuals/blob/master/Attention_Residuals.pdf) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Moonshot AI has introduced Attention Residuals, an improved Transformer architecture technique that delivers 1.25x training efficiency for 48B parameter large language models. The technique has been validated on Moonshot's 48B parameter Kimi Linear model and received positive feedback from prominent AI researcher Andrej Karpathy. This is a novel and practical improvement to the core Transformer architecture that powers most modern large language models, delivering significant efficiency gains with minimal extra overhead. It helps reduce the high computational cost of training large LLMs, which benefits researchers and developers working on scaling up language model performance. Attention Residuals replaces standard fixed residual connections with learned, input-dependent attention aggregation over outputs from all earlier layers, adding less than 4% extra training overhead and no more than 2% additional inference latency. It also mitigates the PreNorm dilution problem by improving gradient flow, and improved the tested 48B model's GPQA-Diamond benchmark score by 7.5 points alongside gains in coding and math capabilities.

telegram · zaihuapd · Mar 16, 09:05

**Background**: Standard Transformer architectures, the backbone of nearly all modern large language models, use fixed residual connections that uniformly combine outputs from previous layers into the current layer's representation. PreNorm is a common design for deep Transformers that places layer normalization before attention layers to stabilize training, but it suffers from the structural issue called PreNorm dilution that harms performance and efficiency. GPQA-Diamond is a challenging benchmark of graduate-level expert questions built to evaluate advanced reasoning capabilities of state-of-the-art large language models.
