## [Music labels sue Anthropic for billions in copyright case](https://www.cnbeta.com.tw/articles/music/1575518.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Sony Music and Warner Music Publishing have filed a copyright infringement lawsuit against AI company Anthropic in a U.S. federal court, accusing it of illegally scraping millions of copyrighted works including song lyrics and books to train its Claude large language models. If the court rules fully in the labels' favor, total damages could reach billions of U.S. dollars. This high-stakes lawsuit could set a critical legal precedent for the entire generative AI industry, and its outcome may reshape the rules for how AI developers legally source training data. A multi-billion dollar ruling against Anthropic would also set a major benchmark for future copyright claims against generative AI firms. The lawsuit names Anthropic's two co-founders as individual defendants, and alleges that a co-founder downloaded over 5 million pirated books via BitTorrent, while Anthropic staff downloaded an additional 2 million works from Pirate Library Mirror and scraped licensed lyrics from music data platforms. This is the latest in a string of copyright cases against Anthropic, which recently settled a publishing industry lawsuit for $1.5 billion.

**Background**: Claude is a series of state-of-the-art large language models developed by Anthropic, a leading generative AI company. BitTorrent is a popular peer-to-peer file sharing protocol that is frequently used to distribute pirated copyrighted content. Pirate Library Mirror is an anonymous project that mirrors shadow libraries, which are online collections that host or link to unlicensed copyrighted material.

## [Tencent open-sources Hy4 LLM preview](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent released and open-sourced the preview version of its next-generation large language model Hy4 on August 28, 2026. The model saw extremely high early usage traffic on the OpenRouter platform, achieved lower caching costs than competing models, and was developed using a novel recursive self-improvement process where the model helped optimize its own training pipeline. This release demonstrates that major AI players are starting to implement practical recursive self-improvement loops in large language model development, which could accelerate the pace of AI innovation moving forward. Its strong early adoption also indicates high community interest in new open-source large models with competitive performance and cost characteristics. The Hy4 preview has 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active parameters, and a context window larger than 1 million tokens, with a caching cost of only 5% compared to the 10-20% caching cost common for other models. In its recursive self-improvement loop, Hy4 itself proposed optimizations, ran experiments, and provided feedback to improve its own training methods, data strategies, evaluation frameworks and low-level operators.

**Background**: OpenRouter is a popular platform that provides a unified API for accessing hundreds of large language models from different developers and providers. Recursive self-improvement is an AI development concept where an AI system contributes to improving its own architecture, training process or capabilities, forming an iterative improvement loop that many researchers believe could accelerate AI progress.

South Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT has selected three consortiums led by SK Telecom, KT, and Kakao to run the government-backed 'AI for All' project, which will offer all South Korean citizens free unlimited-token access to domestically developed large language models by the end of 2026. Closed beta testing for the service will launch in September 2026, with full public rollout planned before the end of the year. This initiative marks a major step forward in building national public AI infrastructure, making advanced domestic AI accessible to all citizens without usage charges or limits. It also accelerates South Korea's goal of building an AI-enabled society and promotes the development of the local AI industry against the backdrop of global AI competition. The South Korean government will provide the three consortiums with 512 NVIDIA B200 chips, and will cover long-term operational costs via subsidies starting from 2027; major Korean tech firm Naver did not participate in this project. The free AI service will integrate with government systems to support public needs including medical appointment booking, housing search, and tax consultation.

**Background**: The 'AI for All' project is part of South Korea's plan to build an 'AI Universal Basic Society' that makes AI tools broadly available to all citizens. NVIDIA B200 is a data center AI GPU based on the Blackwell microarchitecture, which delivers significantly higher AI training and inference performance than previous generation chips for large language model workloads. An unlimited-token service removes typical usage caps on the number of input and output text tokens, allowing users to generate longer text and process larger requests without additional charges.
