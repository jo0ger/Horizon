## [Alibaba's Tongyi Qwen Tops LMSYS Arena Ranking](https://www.aibase.com/zh/news/26396) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Alibaba's new flagship preview model Tongyi Qwen 3.5-Max-Preview has become the highest-ranked Chinese large language model on the authoritative LMSYS LM Arena benchmark, scoring 1464 to rank fifth globally overall. Alibaba has also fully released the entire Qwen 3.5 model family covering 8 parameter sizes from 0.8B to 397B for diverse hardware environments and use cases. This result marks that Chinese general-purpose large language models have officially entered the global first tier of AI capability, representing a key milestone for the development of Chinese-developed LLMs. It also provides a full ecosystem of high-performance LLM options for global developers and enterprises, accelerating the deep deployment of AI applications across industries. LMSYS LM Arena uses anonymous model battles and crowdsourced developer voting, which makes it widely recognized as hard to game and highly valuable for referencing real-world model performance. The 3.5-Max-Preview is a preview release, and Alibaba will collect user feedback to iterate and optimize the upcoming official full version.

telegram · AI_News_CN · Mar 20, 01:10

**Background**: LMSYS LM Arena (also called Chatbot Arena) is an authoritative crowdsourced LLM benchmark organized by the Large Model Systems Organization, which uses anonymous randomized battles to rank models based on real-world user and developer comparisons. Mixture of Experts (MoE) is a widely adopted LLM architecture that splits the full model into multiple small expert sub-networks, only activating a small subset of parameters during each inference. This design allows MoE models to achieve high overall performance from a large total parameter count while keeping inference cost and energy consumption low, making it popular for flagship large LLMs.
